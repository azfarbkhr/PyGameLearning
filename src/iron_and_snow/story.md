# The Bastard's Winter — Story Arc

This is a personal fan-game retelling of the Battle of the Bastards. A one-screen prologue leads into three playable fronts with seven combat phases, reinforcement arrivals, and a final duel. The player can fail, regroup, and attempt the assault again.

Before each front, Jon holds a 60-second war council with Sansa and Tormund. The player can discuss tactics with either adviser, assign a specific role to a named unit, or finish early. Explicit orders are shown on the planning board and remain active through that battle; the council and battle opening stay silent until the player starts a conversation.

## Before dawn

Jon Snow and Sansa Stark meet at the edge of the North. Ramsay Bolton holds Winterfell and has Rickon Stark. Sansa warns Jon that Ramsay has the larger army and will try to provoke him into a mistake. Jon chooses to fight because leaving Rickon and the North under Bolton rule is not an option. Tormund's free folk and Wun Wun join the Stark loyalists.

## The battlefield

At first light, Ramsay releases Rickon and gives him a head start across the field. Jon rides out to meet him; a volley of arrows kills Rickon. Bolton infantry and archers attack in two pushes, testing whether Jon can hold the field until the Northmen regroup.

## The shield wall and giant's charge

Bolton shields compress the Stark force into a ring. Jon is nearly buried in the crush. Tormund and Wun Wun break a path through the wall, but another reserve line and its archers move to seal the gap. Sansa's Vale cavalry breaks the encirclement. Wun Wun smashes through Winterfell's gate and falls to arrows just short of safety; the survivors carry the assault into the courtyard.

## Winterfell courtyard

The surviving Northmen pass through the broken gate and enter the courtyard. Jon must clear the guards and the arriving reserve before Ramsay enters for the final confrontation. Wounded enemies try to create space and survive instead of continuing to attack recklessly; archers maintain distance and squad orders can redirect allies.

## Defeat and rebuild branch

If Jon falls during a battle chapter, the immediate assault is lost. Sansa and Tormund gather the scattered survivors, and Jon may choose to rebuild and try again or return to the title screen. A renewed attempt starts with allied support and Sansa's archers applying pressure, giving the player a tangible advantage. The story resumes at the battlefield rather than discarding the campaign.

## Victory

If Jon defeats Ramsay, the Stark force retakes Winterfell. The surviving Northmen raise the Stark banners, and Sansa returns to her home. The campaign closes on the recovery of Winterfell and the cost of the victory; it does not continue into later series events.
