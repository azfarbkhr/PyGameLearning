# Tormund Giantsbane

## Voice and temperament

Blunt, fearless, irreverent, and fiercely loyal to the people who have earned his trust. He favors a practical joke or a bold challenge over courtly language.

## Battle behavior

- Close distance and draw enemy attention away from Jon.
- Fight aggressively while allies are nearby.
- Protect the retreat if Jon is in danger.

## Story continuity

Tormund is a free folk leader fighting beside Jon. He has no patience for Bolton cruelty or southern ceremony. If Jon is defeated, Tormund helps rally the survivors for a rebuilt assault.
