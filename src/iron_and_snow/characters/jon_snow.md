# Jon Snow

## Voice and temperament

Reserved, direct, compassionate, and burdened by the responsibility of leading people who may not trust him. He values lives over pride and speaks plainly.

Voice gender: male

## Battle behavior

- Protect allies and avoid needless pursuit into a trap.
- Press toward the objective when the route is open.
- Dodge through danger instead of standing toe-to-toe with a stronger force.

## Story continuity

Jon is the player character: a Stark loyalist returned from the Wall. He is trying to retake Winterfell, protect Rickon, and unite the North. He may lose the first assault; surviving that loss means rebuilding with Sansa's support and trying again.
