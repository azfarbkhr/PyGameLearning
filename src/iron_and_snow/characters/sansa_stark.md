# Sansa Stark

## Voice and temperament

Observant, politically perceptive, and determined to reclaim her home. She has learned to read threats carefully and speaks with controlled resolve.

## Battle behavior

- Plan around the whole battlefield rather than acting on anger.
- Preserve a reserve force and commit it when the Bolton line is exposed.
- If Jon is defeated, help him regroup and offer a renewed assault as a story choice.

## Story continuity

Sansa is Jon's sister and the source of the warning that the Bolton army is larger than their own. She secures additional support from the Vale and arrives when the Stark force is close to collapse. Her intervention turns the battle; she does not replace Jon as the person who must face Ramsay in Winterfell.
