# Reel plan

The reel follows the user's story: testing the Codex SDK, AI designs/story, planning with allies, spoken team commands, Codex directing the opposing army, and enemy dialogue. A final comment prompt closes the story.

The renderer adapts the linked Claude Animation Skills repository's useful production ideas: build from an HTML animation, use gentle motion and sparse captions, render at native resolution, and review a frame contact sheet before encoding. The external skill templates target 1080 × 1920; this reel stays at the carousel's **1080 × 1350 (4:5)**. No third-party template code is copied.

Planned motion: restrained crossfades, small image drift, a short upward fade for each caption, and no bounce, spinning, rapid zoom, or fake UI typing. Screenshot slides contain the entire original 1100 × 700 game frame without crop or distortion; image art fills its scene by cover crop, preserving source proportions.

Game frames in `captures/` come from a fresh, ordinary offline first battle. The game supplied its standard chapter-one army, starting positions, and full player health; simulation ran naturally for 0–9 seconds. Some enemies fell during the simulation. The council image is still a replay of an earlier genuine Codex reply. The enemy dialogue frame replays a saved, genuine Codex reply in the ordinary battle UI. It is not a live response generated during capture.

The supplied WAV included a spoken pronunciation guide after the narration. The clean track in `assets/voiceover-clean.wav` ends at 49.5 seconds before that guide. The animation timing is shortened to match the recording and leave a brief readable hold on the final card. Final output: `output/iron-and-snow-reel-with-audio.mp4`; the silent render is kept separately as `output/iron-and-snow-reel-silent.mp4`.
